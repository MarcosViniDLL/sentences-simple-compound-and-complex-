# 🧩 Chapter 5 — Sentence Structure

> *"Understanding clauses is the first step to understanding sentences."*

---

# 🎯 Objective | **Objetivo**

Learn how to identify **Independent Clauses** and **Dependent Clauses**, and understand how they are used to classify sentences as **Simple, Compound,** or **Complex**.

**Aprender a identificar Orações Independentes (Independent Clauses) e Orações Dependentes (Dependent Clauses), além de compreender como elas são utilizadas para classificar frases como Simple, Compound ou Complex.**

---

# 📖 First: What is a Clause? | **Primeiro: o que é uma Clause?**

A **Clause** is a group of words that contains a **Subject** and a **Verb**.

**Uma Clause (oração) é um grupo de palavras que contém um Sujeito (Subject) e um Verbo (Verb).**

Example:

```text
She studies English.
```

- **Subject:** She
- **Verb:** studies
- **Object:** English

---

# 🟢 Independent Clause

An **Independent Clause** expresses a complete idea and can stand alone as a sentence.

**Uma Independent Clause (Oração Independente) expressa uma ideia completa e pode existir sozinha como uma frase.**

### Example

```text
She studies English.
```

A frase possui sentido completo.

Podemos dizer apenas:

> **She studies English.**

E a ideia continua completa.

### Other examples

```text
I like coffee.
```

```text
They went home.
```

```text
John plays soccer.
```

Todas essas são **Independent Clauses**.

---

# 🔴 Dependent Clause

A **Dependent Clause** contains a Subject and a Verb, but **does not express a complete idea by itself**.

**Uma Dependent Clause (Oração Dependente) possui Sujeito e Verbo, mas não expressa uma ideia completa sozinha.**

Ela depende de outra oração para completar seu sentido.

### Example

```text
Because I was tired
```

Observe:

- **Subject:** I
- **Verb:** was
- A ideia, porém, está incompleta.

❌

```text
Because I was tired.
```

A pessoa que lê naturalmente pergunta:

> **What happened because you were tired?**

Agora observe:

```text
Because I was tired, I went to bed.
```

Agora temos:

- **Dependent Clause:** Because I was tired
- **Independent Clause:** I went to bed

A frase possui sentido completo.

---

# 🔎 How to Identify a Clause

## Step 1 — Find the Subject

**Encontre o Subject (Sujeito).**

Pergunte:

> Who? / What?

Example:

```text
The student studies English.
```

**Subject:** The student

---

## Step 2 — Find the Verb

**Encontre o Verb (Verbo).**

Pergunte:

> What is the subject doing?

Example:

```text
The student studies English.
```

**Verb:** studies

---

## Step 3 — Ask: Is the idea complete?

**Pergunte: a ideia está completa?**

### Complete idea:

```text
The student studies English.
```

✅ **Independent Clause**

### Incomplete idea:

```text
Because the student studies English...
```

❌ **Dependent Clause**

---

# 🧠 The Most Important Question

Quando encontrar uma Clause, faça esta pergunta:

> **Can this clause stand alone?**

**Essa oração consegue existir sozinha?**

### YES ✅

→ **Independent Clause**

### NO ❌

→ **Dependent Clause**

---

# 📊 Independent × Dependent Clause

| Independent Clause | Dependent Clause |
|--------------------|------------------|
| Possui Subject + Verb | Possui Subject + Verb |
| Expressa uma ideia completa | Não expressa uma ideia completa |
| Pode existir sozinha | Depende de outra oração |
| Pode ser uma sentença completa | Não é uma sentença completa sozinha |

---

# 📝 Examples | **Exemplos**

### Example 1

```text
I went home.
```

✅ Independent Clause

A ideia está completa.

---

### Example 2

```text
Because I was tired
```

❌ Dependent Clause

A ideia está incompleta.

---

### Example 3

```text
Although she studied hard
```

❌ Dependent Clause

Precisamos de outra oração:

```text
Although she studied hard, she failed the exam.
```

# 🧩 Now: Types of Sentences | **Agora: Tipos de Sentenças**

Depois de identificar as Clauses, podemos descobrir qual é o tipo da **Sentence**.

Existem três tipos importantes neste conteúdo:

- **Simple Sentence**
- **Compound Sentence**
- **Complex Sentence**

---

# 🟢 1. Simple Sentence

A **Simple Sentence** contains **one Independent Clause**.

**Uma Simple Sentence possui uma única Independent Clause.**

### Example

```text
She studies English.
```

Temos:

```text
[Independent Clause]
She studies English.
```

Portanto:

✅ **Simple Sentence**

---

### Other examples

```text
I like music.
```

```text
John plays soccer.
```

```text
The students are studying.
```

Todas possuem apenas **uma Independent Clause**.

---

# 🔵 2. Compound Sentence

A **Compound Sentence** contains **two or more Independent Clauses** connected by a **Coordinating Conjunction**.

**Uma Compound Sentence possui duas ou mais Independent Clauses conectadas por uma Coordinating Conjunction.**

As principais Coordinating Conjunctions são conhecidas pelo mnemônico:

## FANBOYS

- **For**
- **And**
- **Nor**
- **But**
- **Or**
- **Yet**
- **So**

### Example

```text
I was tired, but I studied.
```

Observe:

```text
[I was tired] + but + [I studied]
```

Temos:

- Independent Clause → I was tired
- Coordinating Conjunction → but
- Independent Clause → I studied

Portanto:

✅ **Compound Sentence**

---

# 🔀 Another Example

```text
She studied hard, so she passed the exam.
```

Analisando:

```text
[She studied hard] + so + [she passed the exam]
```

As duas partes possuem sentido completo.

Portanto:

✅ **Compound Sentence**

---

# 🟣 3. Complex Sentence

A **Complex Sentence** contains at least:

- **One Independent Clause**
- **One Dependent Clause**

**Uma Complex Sentence possui pelo menos uma Independent Clause e uma Dependent Clause.**

As duas são conectadas por uma **Subordinating Conjunction**.

---

### Example

```text
Because I was tired, I went to bed.
```

Analisando:

```text
[Because I was tired] + [I went to bed]
```

- **Dependent Clause:** Because I was tired
- **Independent Clause:** I went to bed

Portanto:

✅ **Complex Sentence**

---

# 🔀 Another Example

```text
I stayed home because it was raining.
```

Analisando:

```text
[I stayed home] + [because it was raining]
```

- Independent Clause → I stayed home
- Dependent Clause → because it was raining

Portanto:

✅ **Complex Sentence**

---

# 📊 Simple × Compound × Complex

| Type | Structure | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Simple** | 1 Independent Clause | I study English. |
| **Compound** | 2 Independent Clauses | I study English, and I practice every day. |
| **Complex** | 1 Independent + 1 Dependent Clause | I study English because I want to improve. |

# ⚠️ Common Mistake | **Erro Comum**

Não confunda **Clause** com **Sentence**.

Uma sentence pode possuir mais de uma clause.

Por exemplo:

```text
I was tired, but I studied.
```

Essa é **uma Sentence**, mas possui **duas Independent Clauses**.

# 🎓 What to Remember | **O que lembrar**

- ✅ **Clause** possui Subject + Verb.
- ✅ **Independent Clause** possui sentido completo.
- ✅ **Dependent Clause** não possui sentido completo sozinha.
- ✅ **Simple Sentence** = 1 Independent Clause.
- ✅ **Compound Sentence** = 2 ou mais Independent Clauses.
- ✅ **Complex Sentence** = Independent Clause + Dependent Clause.
- ✅ **FANBOYS** são Coordinating Conjunctions.
- ✅ **Subordinating Conjunctions** introduzem Dependent Clauses.

---

# 💡 Chapter Summary | **Resumo do Capítulo**

Para identificar o tipo de uma frase, primeiro é necessário reconhecer suas **Clauses**. Uma **Independent Clause** apresenta uma ideia completa e pode existir sozinha, enquanto uma **Dependent Clause** precisa de outra oração para completar seu sentido.

Depois de identificar as clauses, podemos classificar a sentença:

**Simple Sentence** possui uma Independent Clause; **Compound Sentence** possui duas ou mais Independent Clauses conectadas por uma Coordinating Conjunction; e **Complex Sentence** possui uma Independent Clause e pelo menos uma Dependent Clause.

Esse processo pode ser resumido em três perguntas:

> **1. Quantas Clauses existem?**

> **2. Elas são Independent ou Dependent?**

> **3. Como elas estão conectadas?**
