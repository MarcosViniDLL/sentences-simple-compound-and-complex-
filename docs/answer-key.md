# 📚 Part 1 — Sentence Structure

## 1. Identify the Subject (S), Verb (V) and Object (O)

### a)

```text
John plays soccer.
```

- Subject: **John**
- Verb: **plays**
- Object: **soccer**

---

### b)

```text
Mary bought a new laptop.
```

- Subject: **Mary**
- Verb: **bought**
- Object: **a new laptop**

---

### c)

```text
The teacher explained the lesson.
```

- Subject: **The teacher**
- Verb: **explained**
- Object: **the lesson**

---

## 2. Classify the sentences

### a)

```text
She studies every day.
```

✅ **Simple Sentence**

Possui apenas uma **Independent Clause**.

---

### b)

```text
She studies every day, and she practices English at night.
```

✅ **Compound Sentence**

Duas **Independent Clauses** ligadas por uma **Coordinating Conjunction (and)**.

---

### c)

```text
Because she studies every day, she improves quickly.
```

✅ **Complex Sentence**

Possui uma **Dependent Clause** ("Because she studies every day") e uma **Independent Clause**.

# 📖 Part 6 — Reading Practice

Texto:

```text
Lucas woke up early because he had an important exam. He studied for two hours, and then he had breakfast. Although he felt nervous, he was confident. When he arrived at school, his friends wished him good luck. He smiled, so everyone felt more relaxed.
```

---

## 12.

### a)

Coordinating Conjunction:

✅ **and**

ou

✅ **so**

---

### b)

Subordinating Conjunctions:

- because
- although
- when

---

### c)

Compound Sentence

```text
He smiled, so everyone felt more relaxed.
```

---

### d)

Complex Sentence

```text
Although he felt nervous, he was confident.
```

Também são aceitas:

```text
Lucas woke up early because he had an important exam.
```

```text
When he arrived at school, his friends wished him good luck.
```

---

### e)

Main Idea

Lucas prepared himself for an important exam, remained confident despite being nervous, and received support from his friends.
