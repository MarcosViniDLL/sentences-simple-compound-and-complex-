    📝 Chapter 5 — Practice Exercises

    "Practice is the key to mastering grammar."

    🎯 Objective | Objetivo

    Apply the concepts studied throughout this material by solving practical exercises involving Sentence Structure, Coordination, Coordinating Conjunctions (FANBOYS) and Subordinating Conjunctions.

    Aplicar os conceitos estudados nesta apostila por meio de exercícios práticos envolvendo Estrutura da Frase, Coordenação, FANBOYS e Conjunções Subordinativas.

    📚 Part 1 — Sentence Structure

    1.

    Identify the Subject (S), Verb (V) and Object (O).

    a)

    John plays soccer.

    b)

    Mary bought a new laptop.

    c)

    The teacher explained the lesson.

    2.

    Classify the sentences.

    • Simple Sentence
    • Compound Sentence
    • Complex Sentence

    a)

    She studies every day.

    b)

    She studies every day, and she practices English at night.

    c)

    Because she studies every day, she improves quickly.

    🤝 Part 2 — Coordination

    3.

    Which sentence contains Coordination?

    A)

    Although it was raining, we went out.

    B)

    I was hungry, so I made a sandwich.

    4.

    Explain the difference between:

    • Coordination
    • Subordination

    🔗 Part 3 — FANBOYS

    5.

    Choose the correct conjunction.

    a)

    I was tired, ______ I went to bed.

    • and
    • but
    • so

    b)

    She likes coffee, ______ she doesn't like tea.

    • but
    • so
    • for

    c)

    Would you like tea ______ coffee?

    • but
    • or
    • so

    d)

    He was hungry, ______ he made dinner.

    • so
    • yet
    • but

    e)

    She studied hard, ______ she failed the exam.

    • yet
    • and
    • so

    6.

    Complete the sentences using the correct FANBOYS conjunction.

    a)

    The movie was long, ______ it was interesting.

    b)

    I can study now, ______ I can study later.

    c)

    She loves music, ______ she also plays the piano.

    d)

    He didn't study, ______ he failed the test.

    🔀 Part 4 — Subordinating Conjunctions

    7.

    Complete the sentences.

    a)

    __ it was raining, we stayed home.

    b)

    I'll call you __ I arrive.

    c)

    You won't pass __ you study.

    d)

    She smiled __ she saw her friend.

    8.

    Choose the correct answer.

    A)

    Because she was sick, she stayed home.

    B)

    She stayed home because she was sick.

    Which sentence is correct?

    • Only A
    • Only B
    • Both

    9.

    Identify the Dependent Clause.

    Although he was tired, he finished the project.

    🧩 Part 5 — Mixed Exercises

    10.

    Choose the correct conjunction.

    a)

    I wanted to travel, ______ I didn't have enough money.

    b)

    She called me ______ she arrived.

    c)

    You can stay here ______ leave now.

    d)

    He was late ______ the traffic was terrible.

    11.

    Rewrite the sentences using one conjunction.

    a)

    I was hungry.

I made a sandwich.

    b)

    She studied hard.

She passed the exam.

    c)

    It was raining.

We stayed inside.

    📖 Part 6 — Reading Practice

    Read the paragraph.

    Lucas woke up early because he had an important exam. He studied for two hours, and then he had breakfast. Although he felt nervous, he was confident. When he arrived at school, his friends wished him good luck. He smiled, so everyone felt more relaxed.

    12.

    Answer the questions.

    a)

    Find one Coordinating Conjunction.

    b)

    Find two Subordinating Conjunctions.

    c)

    Identify one Compound Sentence.

    d)

    Identify one Complex Sentence.

    e)

    What is the main idea of the paragraph?

    📝 Challenge

    Rewrite the paragraph using different conjunctions whenever possible without changing its meaning.

    💡 Study Tip | Dica de Estudo

    Ao resolver os exercícios, tente identificar primeiro a relação entre as ideias:

    • Adição?

    • Contraste?

    • Consequência?

    • Causa?

    • Tempo?

    Isso facilita muito a escolha da conjunction correta.
